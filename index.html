📖 How to Play

  • Events: Keep an eye out for Random Events! Good or bad things can happen every 20-30 seconds.
  • Evolutions: Keep your pet alive to see them grow! (Stage 2 at 45s, Epic Stage at 120s). Evolved pets earn more coins!
  • Vital Stats: Keep Hunger, Happiness, Energy, and Cleanliness above 0%.
  • Sickness: Sickness drains stats faster and can kill. Never give medicine to a healthy pet!
